This dataset is used in the research paper GRAGLLM: GraphRAG-Enhanced Large Language Models for Explainable Multi-Aspect Sentiment Analysis.
The dataset was collected via web scraping from two prominent platforms: Skytrax and TripAdvisor. It comprises comprehensive reviews for major Indian airlines, capturing a wide range of passenger experiences and sentiment expressions.
The dataset includes reviews from the following airlines:

Air India
IndiGo
Vistara
SpiceJet
Air India Express

Each review is enriched with metadata such as travel class, route, traveler type, date, and includes both aspect-specific feedback and emotional signals aligned with Ekman’s model of emotions.
